Thank you for reviewing Unhook.

FREE, NO LOGIN: Unhook is completely free (no in-app purchases, no ads) and has no accounts and no server. No demo account is needed. All data is stored on the device with SwiftData.

QUICK TEST (about 3 minutes)
1. Onboarding: pick a habit (for example Vaping), choose "Just now", enter a rough daily cost, pick reasons or tap "Skip for now", see the one-year projection, then choose "Maybe later" or turn on reminders.
2. Today tab: the dawn orb shows the live streak. Below it are today's three missions (tap one to check it off), the 30-Day Reset challenge card, the daily pledge, the habit list and a tip of the day.
3. Tap "Craving? Tap for SOS" at the bottom of Today for the 60-second guided breathing exercise.
4. Tap "Talk to your coach" on Today. On devices without Apple Intelligence the coach uses replies built into the app; on supported devices it uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Messages never leave the device. Messages that mention self-harm get a fixed, caring reply with a link to findahelpline.com.
5. Tap the 30-Day Reset card (or open unhook://challenge/reset30) to see the challenge that our in-app event "30-Day Reset" links to.
6. Progress tab: rank and XP, a calendar of clean days, charts, craving insights and achievements.
7. Settings > Meet the founder: the developer's story, his Instagram and his other apps (opens in Instagram / the App Store).

HEALTH CONTENT: Unhook is a self-help tool. The recovery timeline uses cautious wording based on WHO, CDC and NHS milestones and says it is general information, not medical advice. The app links to findahelpline.com for free crisis support.

PRIVACY: no data collection, no tracking, no third-party SDKs. Face ID is used only for the optional app lock.
