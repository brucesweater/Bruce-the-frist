Quit smoking, vaping, drinking, porn, gambling, sugar, doomscrolling or anything else, one day at a time. Unhook counts every second you stay free, shows the money and time you win back, and gets you through cravings with a 60-second breathing exercise and a private coach.

Unhook is 100% free. No ads, no account, no tracking, nothing to buy. Everything stays on your iPhone.

WATCH THE NIGHT TURN INTO DAY
Your streak lives in a glowing dawn orb that brightens as you get closer to your next milestone. Days, hours, minutes and seconds tick up live, next to the money saved and the time you've reclaimed.

BEAT EVERY CRAVING
When a craving hits, tap SOS for 60 seconds of guided breathing, with your own reasons for quitting on screen. Then note what set it off, so you can see your patterns later.

A COACH THAT'S ALWAYS AWAKE
Talk a craving through with a private coach. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence it uses Apple's on-device model; on every other iPhone it uses replies built into the app. Your messages never leave your phone.

A REASON TO COME BACK EVERY DAY
- Daily pledge: commit to one clean day, with a quick mood check-in
- Daily missions: three small, doable actions each day (a glass of water, a walk, a message to a friend)
- Ranks: earn XP for every clean day, pledge, beaten craving and mission, and rise from Midnight to Legend. A slip never takes your progress away.
- The 30-Day Reset: one small step a day for 30 days, with a fresh start any time you want one

SEE HOW FAR YOU'VE COME
- 13 milestones, from 1 hour to 5 years, with a celebration for each
- A recovery timeline for every habit (smoking follows WHO, CDC and NHS milestones)
- A calendar of your clean days, craving insights ("cravings peak around 9 PM"), mood charts and achievements
- Savings goals: watch the money you don't spend pay for something you love

KIND WHEN YOU SLIP
Slipped? Log it and start a fresh streak. Your earlier clean time stays banked, and you can undo a slip logged by mistake.

MADE FOR YOUR DAY
- Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, including a one-tap SOS widget
- Siri and Shortcuts: "Open SOS in Unhook", "Check my streak in Unhook"
- Face ID lock that also hides what you're quitting in widgets and notifications
- Gentle reminders, haptics, four app icons, data export (JSON or CSV)
